## Development sequence and decision gates

| Sequence | Workstream | Required outcome before proceeding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Existing MVP correction | SFT-01 freshness lifecycle first, then SZ-01/02/03, PLN-01 and current documentation/contracts; no UI redesign |

| 3 | Authentication/authorization | AUTH-01 optional identity and AUTH-02 server/tenant roles after threat/privacy model; retain anonymous SOS |
| 4 | Persistent profiles | PRF-01 consent/local versioning/optional cloud sync, DB-01 design; account persistence is new startup/original P2 scope |
| 5 | Safe Zone verification/moderation | SZ-04 operator authority/history and SZ-05 authorized isolated writes; genuine verified resources, never promoted DEMO |
| 6 | Enterprise backend/workflows | ENT-01 only after agreed contracts and stable worker flows; practical interventions via current engine |
| 7 | Notifications | NOT-01 opt-in, expiry/timezone and failure semantics; optional P2 |
| 8 | PWA/offline | PWA-01 with strict stale-advisory behavior and emergency text; optional P2 |
| 9 | Advanced intelligence | ADV-01 expert-led evidence, not speculative threshold edits; optional research |
| 10 | Production readiness | DEP-01/SEC-03/OBS-01/TST-01/PERF-04/OPS-01 verified on approved staging; no audit-time public deployment |
| 11 | Controlled pilot | SAF-01 and PIL-01 expert/partner approval, consent/stop criteria and independent release review |

Some security, documentation and research planning can proceed independently; the dependency column governs actual rollout. Do not interpret sequence as permission to postpone a security prerequisite until after exposure. Clinical review starts early and gates pilot use. Availability contract decisions precede implementation; engine rule changes require separate qualified validation.

## Estimation

S roughly 1–2 engineering days; M 3–5; L 1–2 engineering weeks; XL research/cross-team scope exceeding two weeks or not estimable until decisions. These are rough complexity aids, **not delivery promises**; security approval, operational partners, device access, clinical review and data collection can dominate elapsed time. Re-estimate after discovery. No unsupported staffing or startup deadline is invented.

## Mandatory versus optional scope

Mandatory worker MVP corrections: SFT-01, SZ-01/02/03, PLN-01, LOC-01 critical text, CON-01/API-01 and DOC-01; safe community enablement requires SZ-04/05 rather than bypassing the disabled flag. Before startup/pilot exposure: SEC-01/02/03, SAF-01, LIC-01, appropriate test/operations gates and real verified resource governance. Authentication, cloud persistence and enterprise are startup expansion with original MVP/P1/P2 provenance explicitly identified; they are not retroactive requirements for the submitted anonymous hackathon.

Optional: PLN-02 expanded schedules, PERF-03 optimizations after measurement, NOT-01, PWA-01, ADV-01. DEMO-01 is informational preservation, not a feature task. No optional feature should delay freshness/security/safety fixes. See [ranked backlog](PRIORITIZED_BACKLOG.md) for immediate handoff.
